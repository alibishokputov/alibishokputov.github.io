Teaching

I teach graduate students to choose, run, and defend quantitative methods for urban policy questions, using R and reproducible workflows.

Teaching Statement (PDF)

Approach

  • Make analytical choices visible. Students explain why they chose a measure or a spatial boundary and where their conclusions stop.
  • Feedback while there is time to revise. Semester-long projects with milestones, so I can respond to the reasoning behind an analysis.
  • Worked examples, then transfer. I change the data or geographic scale to see how students respond when the example no longer supplies every answer.
  • Every discipline at the table. Early exercises and rotating roles in group projects build technical confidence alongside substantive insight.
  • Responsible AI use. Permitted uses are explicit, assistance is disclosed, and students show how they verified its output.
  • Work with a life beyond the report. Conference submissions, publications, or analyses for community partners where the question warrants it.
