Research

Dissertation

"Telework and the Changing Urban Constraint: Housing, Home-Work Separation, and Uneven Commute Exposure"

Advisor: Casey Dawkins  ·  University of Maryland

Commuting cost has traditionally driven where households and firms locate. Telework loosens that constraint, redistributing housing costs, home-work separation, and commute exposure unevenly across the workforce. The dissertation uses Maryland as a case study, combining commuter survey data, administrative records, and spatial econometric methods.
